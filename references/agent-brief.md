## C5 Relationship Confession — Proven Structure

1. Provocative relational situation, taboo-adjacent not explicit
2. Pattern-interrupt reframe ("and I'm not even mad")
3. Before-state (vulnerability)
4. Reversal ("now I want it more than him")
5. Credit the product casually
6. Hold product at second 10–11 minimum — never before second 9, not even passively resting in frame
7. Affectionate product interaction preferred (kiss, press to cheek) — destigmatises without words
8. Hard CTA: "Link below. Faster! Don't wait!" + direct point at camera

**Framing:** ECU face from second 0. Avatar reaches toward camera in opening 2 seconds. No wide establishing shots.

**Expression arc (C5):** tired knowing smirk → explosive laugh → soft vulnerability → self-surprise → warm affection → urgent aggression

## Format Rules

- **3 approved formats:** Confession / Bold Admission / Boyfriend-Gift
- **Product reveal:** never before second 9. "No product before Xs" alone is insufficient for Seedance — add "product not visible in any form before Xs"
- **Script length:** 40–80 words total. Count it. Cut weakest line if over 80.
- **Line length:** no line over 12 words
- **CTA:** 4–6 words max. Hard for C5 and Testimonial. Soft for Problem-Solution and Skit.

## Voice Rules (hard — non-negotiable)

1. First line = hook. No warmup.
2. No transitions: no "honestly", "you know what", "like I said", "basically"
3. No three-part parallel sentences
4. "Oh! Venus" in dialogue; "Oh Venus" everywhere else
5. Write it the way she'd text a friend at 11pm — if it sounds written, rewrite it
6. Short sentences carry the rhythm — one idea per line
7. Don't instruct Manglish explicitly — short sentences produce natural cadence

## Copy Principle

Precise enough that the audience infers correctly; vague enough that the algorithm doesn't flag it.
- Never name the product function — say "this", "it", "the one from Oh Venus"
- Never name the outcome explicitly — "changed things between us" > "improved our sex life"
- Use relationship framing — human AND algorithm-safe

## CTA Rules

- Hard urgency for C5 and Testimonial formats: "Link below. Faster! Don't wait!" + pointing at camera
- Soft for Problem-Solution and Skit: "Save this. Link's below. You'll love it."
- **Eye rule:** CTA is warm-confident. Eyes stay soft. Smile stays. No wide-eye surprise.
- Soft insider CTAs ("懂的都懂") confirmed to underperform with high-energy arcs

---

## Seedance Production Rules

- Single continuous prompt — never split into clips. MANUAL mode only, never AUTO.
- Block order: SUBJECT → continuous scene arc → NEGATIVE PROMPTS
- Malaysian English spec (mandatory): "delivered in Malaysian English — warm, casual, slightly accented, natural rhythm, like talking to a friend" + "no American or British accent"
- Pauses must say "mandatory" or Seedance rushes them
- Lock expression to emotional arc — use "mandatory" for key beats
- Product control: "product not visible in any form before Xs" + "product enters frame only between Xs–Ys, immediately exits"
- No timestamped blocks — describe as continuous narrative with "then," "as she," "before finally"
- Never use AUTO mode — it smooths delivery beats
