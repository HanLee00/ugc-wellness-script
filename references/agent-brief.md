## C5 Relationship Confession — Proven Structure

1. Provocative relational situation, taboo-adjacent not explicit
2. Pattern-interrupt reframe ("and I'm not even mad")
3. Before-state (vulnerability)
4. Reversal ("now I want it more than him")
5. Credit the product casually
6. Product **physically absent from frame** before second 9 — not just "not held." Resting in foreground out-of-focus still triggers ad-brain.
7. Affectionate product interaction preferred (kiss, press to cheek)
8. Hard CTA: "Link below. Faster! Don't wait!" + direct point at camera

**Framing:** ECU face from second 0. Avatar reaches toward camera in opening 2 seconds. No wide shots.

**Expression arc:** tired knowing smirk → explosive laugh → soft vulnerability → self-surprise → warm affection → urgent aggression

## Format Rules

- **3 approved formats:** Confession / Bold Admission / Boyfriend-Gift
- **BROAD batch adds:** Relationship Pain (EN proven), ZH Self-Reliance, ZH Travel, Male POV/Gifting, ZH Drama
- **Product reveal:** never before second 9. For Seedance: "product not visible in any form before Xs"
- **Script length:** 40–80 words. Count it. Cut weakest line if over 80.
- **CTA:** 4–6 words max. Hard urgency for C5 and high-energy formats.

## Voice Rules (hard)

1. First line = hook. No warmup.
2. No transitions: no "honestly", "you know what", "like I said", "basically"
3. No three-part parallel sentences
4. "Oh! Venus" in dialogue; "Oh Venus" everywhere else
5. Write it the way she'd text a friend at 11pm
6. Short sentences carry the rhythm — one idea per line
7. Don't instruct Manglish explicitly — short sentences produce natural cadence

## Copy Principle

Precise enough that the audience infers correctly; vague enough that the algorithm doesn't flag it.
- Never name the product function — say "this", "it", "the one from Oh Venus"
- Never name the outcome explicitly — "changed things between us" > "improved our sex life"
- Use relationship framing — human AND algorithm-safe

## CTA Rules

- Hard urgency for C5, Testimonial, Relationship Pain formats: "Link below. Faster! Don't wait!" + pointing at camera
- Soft for Problem-Solution and Skit: "Save this. Link's below. You'll love it."
- Coded insider CTAs ("懂的都懂") — valid for ZH awareness creatives only, not conversion-intent ads
- Eyes stay soft. Smile stays. No wide-eye surprise in CTA beat.

## Seedance Production Rules

- Single continuous prompt — never split into clips. MANUAL mode only.
- Block order: SUBJECT → continuous scene arc → NEGATIVE PROMPTS
- Malaysian English spec: "delivered in Malaysian English — warm, casual, slightly accented, natural rhythm, like talking to a friend" + "no American or British accent"
- Pauses must say "mandatory" or Seedance rushes them
- Product control: "product not visible in any form before Xs" + "product enters frame only between Xs–Ys, immediately exits"
- No timestamped blocks — describe as continuous narrative ("then," "as she," "before finally")
- ZH batch currently 720p — upgrade to 4K after conversion data confirms angle

## Compliance Flags (check every script before final output)

- No anatomical terminology → use "C-Spot" / "internal"
- No unqualified efficacy % claims
- No waterproof/IPX claims unless product is rated
- "Designed for Asian women" → "designed for a comfortable fit" on Western platforms
- 爽度 is high-risk in Mandarin paid placements
- 自己开始玩 and 不放过我 flagged by Seedance — use softened alternatives
- Meta banned: "vibrator", "sex toy", "orgasm"
- Test: 60-year-old moderator — if only inference makes it understood, it passes
